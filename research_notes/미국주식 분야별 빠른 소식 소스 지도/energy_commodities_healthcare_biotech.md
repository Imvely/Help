## (A1) Energy trade press & newsletters — who they are, cadence, price, delivery, lead time

### Takeaway
The highest-value paid energy sources for a retail investor split into (i) cheap individual newsletters with published prices (Doomberg ~$300/yr, OilPrice Global Energy Alert $279/yr, Heatmap metered/paywalled, Timmerman-style tiers) and (ii) enterprise wire/price-reporting agencies (Argus, S&P Global Platts/Commodity Insights, Energy Intelligence, NGI, Kpler, Vortexa, Rystad, Wood Mackenzie) that do not publish individual prices and sell by seat/product via sales teams. The OPEC+ "scoop" pipeline is Reuters' sourced reports (bylines Ghaddar/Lawler/Astakhova) typically landing a day or hours before the ministerial meeting, with Bloomberg's Javier Blas (Opinion) and ex-Energy Intelligence/Kpler's Amena Bakr as the most-followed individual OPEC voices.

### Inferences
- For a retail investor, the realistic paid stack is Doomberg (~$300) + OilPrice GEA ($279) + a Heatmap membership; everything else (Argus, Platts, EI, NGI, Kpler, Vortexa, Rystad, WoodMac) is effectively enterprise-priced and reaches retail second-hand via Reuters/Bloomberg citations.
- "Lead time" in oil is dominated by Reuters/Bloomberg sourced reports that land roughly 24 hours to a few hours before the OPEC+ ministerial call (Nov 1, 2026 and Oct 5, 2025 examples). Trade press (Argus, Platts, EI) often has the same timing but behind paywalls; newsletters like Doomberg are analysis, not scoops.
- Amena Bakr's move to Kpler means her OPEC+ sourcing now surfaces through Kpler webinars/notes and her personal X account rather than a news wire; X remains the fastest free channel for her and Javier Blas.

## (A2) Primary energy/commodity data releases that beat the press — exact schedules

### Takeaway
The weekly US energy data clock is: API Tuesday ~4:30 pm ET (paid, $3,193/yr via LSEG/ICE) → EIA WPSR Wednesday 10:30 am ET (free) → EIA gas storage Thursday 10:30 am ET (free) → Baker Hughes rig count Friday 1:00 pm ET (free) → CFTC COT Friday 3:30 pm ET (free, moving to twice weekly by end-2026). Monthly: OPEC MOMR (mid-month, ~4:00 am ET), IEA OMR (10:00 Paris), USDA WASDE (12:00 noon ET, 12 fixed dates).

### Cited Findings

**EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report (WPSR)**
- Core summary and tables post after 10:30 a.m. ET on Wednesdays, with remaining files after 1:00 p.m. ET; holiday weeks shift release by a day, per EIA's schedule page — [EIA What's New](https://www.eia.gov/about/new/index.php?r=149640); official schedule page: https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/schedule.php (fetch blocked this session)
- Moves: XOM, CVX, COP, OXY, DVN, refiners (VLO, MPC, PSX), USO/XLE/XOP.

**API Weekly Statistical Bulletin (WSB)**
- API's 2025 and 2024 Data Products catalogs list the single-subscriber price at $3,193.00 (product T00006, layout ambiguous but consistently that figure), published weekly on Tuesday afternoon (Wednesday after a Monday federal holiday) — [API 2025 catalog PDF](https://www.energytomorrow.org/-/media/Files/Publications/2025-Catalog/09_Data%20Products_2025.pdf); [API 2024 catalog](https://API.org/-/media/Files/Publications/2024-Catalog/2024-Data-Products.pdf)
- API sells the WSB only through authorized redistributors LSEG (ex-Refinitiv) and ICE — [API WSB page](https://www.api.org/energy-insights/statistics/wsb)
- Third-party calendars give the release time as 4:30 pm ET Tuesday — [Babypips](https://www.babypips.com/forexpedia/api-weekly-statistical-bulletin-wsb); [EnergyExch calendar](https://www.energyexch.com/calendar/754-us-api-weekly-statistical-bulletin)
- Practical note: headline API numbers leak onto X/financial Twitter within minutes of 4:30 pm ET (free, after-hours crude futures react) **[UNVERIFIED but widely observed]**.

**EIA Weekly Natural Gas Storage Report**
- Released Thursdays at 10:30 a.m. ET except certain holiday weeks (e.g., Thanksgiving week moved to Wednesday 12:00 p.m. ET in 2025); companion Natural Gas Storage Dashboard posts at 2:30 p.m. ET — [EIA schedule](https://ir.eia.gov/ngs_test/schedule.html); [EIA Information Releases](https://ir.eia.gov/); [EIA What's New](https://www.eia.gov/about/new/?r=50940)
- Moves: EQT, EXE, AR, RRC, LNG, VG, UNG; also utility/IPP gas-burn names.

**Baker Hughes rig count**
- Weekly, Friday 1:00 pm ET (noon Central); released a day early when Friday is a holiday (e.g., Good Friday 2022, Thanksgiving 2016) — [MQL5 calendar](https://mql5.com/en/economic-calendar/united-states/baker-hughes-us-total-rig-count); [investingLive](https://investinglive.com/news/baker-hughes-oil-rig-count-rises-2-to-548-20220414/)
- Moves: SLB, HAL, BKR, oil-services ETF OIH.

**CFTC Commitments of Traders (COT)**
- Standard release Friday 3:30 p.m. ET with Tuesday data; federal holidays may delay 1–2 days — [CFTC release schedule](https://cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/ReleaseSchedule/index.htm); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commitments_of_Traders)
- CFTC press release 9316-26 (Oct 27, 2026) describes a plan to move COT to twice weekly by end of 2026: Monday data published Wednesday, Wednesday data published Friday, both at 3:30 p.m. ET — [CFTC Release 9316-26](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9316-26)

**OPEC Monthly Oil Market Report (MOMR)**
- 2026 release dates per third-party calendar: Jan 14, Feb 11, Mar 11, Apr 13, May 13, Jun 11, Jul 13, Aug 12, Sep 10, Oct 13, Nov 11, Dec 14; a second calendar lists ~4:00 AM (ET) release — [Trading Economics OPEC calendar](https://tradingeconomics.com/opec/calendar/1000); [Catacal](https://www.catacal.com/catalyst/opec-monthly-oil-market-report-1)

### Inferences

## (B1) Biopharma trade press & newsletters — price, cadence, who writes, delivery

### Takeaway
STAT+ ($399/yr list, $39/mo, frequent 50%-off promos) and Endpoints Insider ($200/yr) are the two must-have paid biotech news subscriptions for a retail investor; Timmerman Report ($25/mo) is the affordable VC/deal-focused third. BioCentury, Citeline's Pink Sheet/Scrip, and Evaluate are enterprise-priced (quote only). Adam Feuerstein (STAT) remains the single most market-moving biotech journalist, with a documented history of stock reactions to his pieces and a weekly "Adam's Biotech Scorecard" newsletter inside STAT+.
