## KQ1. Policy / geopolitical intelligence shops (Eurasia Group/GZERO, RANE, Capital Alpha, Beacon Policy Advisors, Signum, Observatory Group, Medley, Teneo, MacroPolo, Rhodium, Trivium, Sinocism, ChinaTalk, Pekingnology, The Wire China, Caixin, SCMP, Nikkei Asia)

### Takeaway
This tier splits into (a) institutional "political intelligence" shops that sell only to funds/corporates at undisclosed, bespoke prices (Eurasia Group, Capital Alpha, Beacon Policy Advisors, Signum, Observatory Group, Medley/Energy Aspects, Teneo, Rhodium) and (b) individually purchasable China newsletters/media where a retail investor can actually subscribe for roughly $150–$250/yr (Sinocism, Trivium, The Wire China, ChinaTalk, Caixin Global) or free (Pekingnology, GZERO, ISW). MacroPolo has shut down. Their edge is interpretation and lead-time on *policy direction* (weeks), not minute-level breaking news; the breaking-news edge sits in KQ2–KQ3.

### Inferences
- For a retail investor, the realistic "China stack" is Sinocism (~$218/yr new-subscriber rate if the last-announced increase stands) + Trivium ($188/yr) + Pekingnology (free) + Caixin Global (~$200–250/yr). Together these cover Politburo/State Council signaling (BABA/PDD/JD/BIDU/NIO regulatory risk), MOFCOM countermeasures (rare earths → defense, autos; export controls → NVDA/AMD/LRCX/AMAT), and tariff retaliation (AAPL/NKE/TSLA).

## KQ2. Washington trade / sanctions "fast lanes" (Politico Pro Trade, Inside U.S. Trade, Punchbowl, Axios Pro, Semafor Principals, Export Compliance Daily, Law360, BIS Federal Register / public inspection, OFAC Recent Actions, USTR releases, Truth Social/X posts of officials)

### Takeaway
The fastest market-moving trade/sanctions "source" in 2025–26 is the President's own Truth Social feed (documented multi-trillion-dollar index moves within minutes), followed by the primary government pages (OFAC Recent Actions, BIS/Federal Register) which move oil and semis on publication. Professional trade newsletters (Politico Pro ~$3,000–$5,000+/seat/yr; Inside U.S. Trade and Export Compliance Daily unpriced/quote-only) add lead time on *rulemaking in progress*, while Punchbowl ($40/mo Premium, $1,200/yr Premium+) covers Hill process. Axios Pro Policy has been discontinued.

### Inferences
- Speed ranking for a retail trader on trade/sanctions news: (1) Truth Social/X posts of Trump and cabinet officials (minutes; algorithmic traders already parse them); (2) OFAC Recent Actions and BIS press releases/Federal Register public inspection (same day, often pre-market or mid-session); (3) wire flashes (Reuters/Bloomberg) typically within 1–5 minutes of the above; (4) trade dailies (ECD, Inside U.S. Trade, Politico Pro Trade) same-day with context and days-to-weeks lead on *pending* rules via interagency-review leaks; (5) general press.
- Because Punchbowl Premium is $40/mo and ECD/Inside Trade/Politico Pro are thousands per seat, the only "pro" fast lane realistically affordable to retail is Punchbowl (Hill process: tariff legislation, Russia sanctions bills, NDAA China provisions).
- Sector mapping: OFAC Russia energy actions → oil majors/tankers; BIS semiconductor rules → NVDA/AMD/LRCX/AMAT/KLAC/ASML; Truth Social tariff posts → AAPL/NKE/autos (GM/F/TSLA)/retailers (WMT/TGT)/broad indices; USTR Section 301/232 notices → steel/aluminum (X, NUE), shipbuilding/shipping (ZIM via port fees).

## KQ3. Conflict trackers that beat wire services (OSINT X accounts, Telegram, ISW, Liveuamap, ACLED, Bellingcat; Middle East: Al-Monitor, Amwaj, Axios/Barak Ravid, Times of Israel liveblog, Al Jazeera, Al Arabiya, Iran International)
