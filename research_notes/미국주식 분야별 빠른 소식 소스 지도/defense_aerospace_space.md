# Defense, Aerospace & Space: fastest/most authoritative specialist news sources for US-listed-equity investors

Research date: 2026-11-08. Method note: findings below come from web-search result pages (search summaries + the linked pages' snippets). Direct page fetches of breakingdefense.com, defensedaily.com and pro.payloadspace.com were blocked by the session's network allow-list, and the shared web-search budget ran out before the final gap-filling batch (ownership of TWZ/DefenseScoop/Air & Space Forces Magazine, SpaceNews/Ars pricing, Orbital Index, FCC-filing-driven ASTS moves, sell-side analysts, The Merge/War on the Rocks, Korean sources). Items in "Gaps" marked **UNVERIFIED (background knowledge)** are from the researcher's prior knowledge and were NOT confirmed this session; the report writer should flag them as such or verify in a follow-up.

Important housekeeping fact for the whole catalog: the Pentagon's web domain has moved. The daily contract-announcement list now lives at **war.gov** (Department of War), the successor to defense.gov — [war.gov/News/Contracts/Search](https://war.gov/News/Contracts/Search). Any "defense.gov/News/Contracts" bookmark should be re-pointed.

## Key Question 1: Which outlets break Pentagon budget, contract-award and program news first (Breaking Defense, Defense One, Defense News, Inside Defense, Janes, Aviation Week, The War Zone, Politico Pro Defense, Punchbowl, DefenseScoop, Air & Space Forces Magazine)?

### Takeaway
The paid "insider" tier (Inside Defense, Politico Pro Defense, Punchbowl Premium+ Defense, Defense Daily, AWIN, Janes) and the free Breaking Defense are where pre-decisional program/budget news surfaces; Breaking Defense and Inside Defense are the two outlets whose stories most often carry "has learned"/"is reporting" attribution in secondary coverage. Defense News (Sightline/Regent) has been hollowed out by 2024-25 layoffs and is now less of a first-mover than it was.

### Inferences
- The speed hierarchy for Pentagon program news is roughly: (1) paid Hill/Pentagon insider products (Politico Pro Defense, Punchbowl Premium+, Inside Defense) and Breaking Defense for pre-decisional memos and budget leaks; (2) Defense Daily/Defense One/DefenseScoop/Air & Space Forces Magazine same-day; (3) Reuters/Bloomberg pick up the big-ticket items (often citing the trade outlet); (4) general press. The Army ATI case (Army "pre-decisional" no-comment while trade press had the memo) is a concrete instance of this lag.
- Breaking Defense is the best free source for a retail investor: it is still hiring (Asia bureau 2025), is free, and is the outlet most often cited by aggregators as "is reporting." Defense News' reduced newsroom (15 US journalists) lowers its scoop frequency; treat it as a reliable reference (Top 100, budget tables) more than a first-mover.
- Inside Defense's ~$1,230-1,295 per-title price and Punchbowl Premium+ ($1,200/yr) are in retail-investor reach; Politico Pro (~$3,000/seat, enterprise-negotiated) and AWIN/Janes (quote-based, four- to six-figure institutional deals) are not, except Janes' $888/yr magazine.

### Gaps
- Could not verify ownership of The War Zone (TWZ), DefenseScoop, Air & Space Forces Magazine or Defense Daily's publisher (search budget exhausted). **UNVERIFIED (background knowledge):** TWZ is owned by Recurrent Ventures and is free; DefenseScoop is Scoop News Group (free, email newsletter); Air & Space Forces Magazine is published by the Air & Space Forces Association (free; very fast on USAF/USSF program news, e.g., budget documents and Space Force awards); Defense Daily is published by Access Intelligence (paid, daily, ~four-figure USD/yr).
- No current Defense Daily price was extractable; no Politico Pro Defense list price exists publicly.
- No source directly documenting a stock move attributable to a Breaking Defense/Inside Defense scoop (e.g., OSK/GD on the ATI memo) was found; the Newsquawk Golden Dome headline is the closest evidence of trader-channel propagation.
- No systematic evidence on "how many hours before Reuters" each outlet runs; only anecdotal attribution patterns.

## Key Question 2: Which space-industry sources are fastest (SpaceNews, Payload, Ars Technica/Eric Berger, NASASpaceflight, The Orbital Index, Quilty Space, Space Intel Report)?

### Takeaway
Payload (free daily, ~11-15k+ subscribers at last count; invitation-only Pro) and SpaceNews (Pocket Ventures; merged with Multiverse Media Oct 2023) are the daily backbone; NASASpaceflight's paid L2 forum ($19.99 per 2 months entry tier) and Space Intel Report (paid, de Selding) are the places where launch/FAA and satellite-operator contract detail appears earliest; Quilty is institutional research (rival-estimated $5k-50k/yr) rather than news.

## Key Question 3: What primary/official feeds beat the press entirely (DoD contract announcements, SAM.gov, GAO bid protests, DSCA FMS notifications, FCC/FAA filings, Congress markups)?

### Takeaway
The single most useful primary feed is the Pentagon's daily contracts list, now at war.gov, posted at 5 p.m. ET each business day (threshold $7.5M) — deliberately after market close by DoD policy, so it moves stocks at the next open; GAO dockets, DSCA press releases/Federal Register notices and GovConWire aggregation are the other high-signal official or near-official streams.

### Cited Findings
- **DoD/DoW daily contracts:** "Contracts valued at $7.5 million or more are announced each business day at 5 p.m." on war.gov (successor to defense.gov). — [war.gov Contracts](https://war.gov/News/Contracts/Search)
- The 5 p.m. release is policy: "The military waits until the financial markets are closed so that the news does not cause one bidder's stock to soar and another's to tumble," per a DoD spokesman (context: 2007 episode where Rep. Wicker's early KC-X/tanker-related disclosure moved shares). — [CFO.com](https://www.cfo.com/risk-compliance/2007/06/did-rep-wicker-move-the-stock-market/)
- Regulatory basis for award announcements: DFARS/AFFARS 5305.303 "Announcement of Contract Awards." — [acquisition.gov 5305.303](https://www.acquisition.gov/index%2Ephp/daffars/5305.303-announcement-contract-awards)
- Tooling: Visualping publishes a how-to for turning the 5 p.m. list into change alerts ("5 p.m. Eastern, each business day. No postings on weekends or federal holidays"). — [Visualping](https://visualping.io/blog/dod-contracts-awarded)
- Zacks and similar outlets write day-after roundups ("LMT and BA secured a number of notable deals from the Department of Defense's daily funding session"), confirming the list is a standing input to equity commentary. — [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/1242419/{https:/www.zackstrade.com/2019-1-dollar})
- **Contract-award aggregators (near-primary):** GovConWire (Executive Mosaic) runs a dedicated contract-awards category and search by agency/company (e.g., Shield AI, DoD), and covers State Dept/DSCA approvals ("State Dept approves proposed $780M sale of Stinger missiles to 3 NATO countries" — RTX). — [GovConWire contract awards](https://www.govconwire.com/category/contract-awards/page/12); [GovConWire DSCA Stinger](https://www.govconwire.com/article/state-dept-approves-proposed-780m-sale-of-stinger-missiles-to-3-nato-countries); [ExecutiveGov DoD category](https://www.executivegov.com/articles/category/dod/page/142)
- **Paid contract-data platforms:** HigherGov (federal contract records searchable by award ID) and Govly (vendor/opportunity pages) index SAM.gov/FPDS data. — [HigherGov record example](https://www.highergov.com/contract/03310319D0071-20341423F00010); [Govly vendor page](https://app.govly.com/public/vendors/quilty_analytics)
- **DSCA / FMS notifications:** Section 36(b) arms-sale notifications are formally published in the Federal Register (govinfo), e.g., multiple 2025-26 notices. — [Federal Register, Jan 15, 2026](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-01-15/html/2026-00712.htm); [Federal Register, Dec 12, 2025](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-12-12/pdf/2025-22600.pdf)
- DSCA notices name the "principal contractor," which determines the ticker beneficiary: one January report noted Lockheed was sole principal contractor on a PATRIOT deal though RTX is involved; a Singapore torpedo sale was from Pentagon stockpiles, "meaning no extra revenue for RTX." Notifications "are not guarantees of sales." — [AOL/Reuters arms deals Jan](https://www.aol.com/articles/u-international-arms-deals-surge-102500075.html); [Stockopedia RTX news](https://www.stockopedia.com/share-prices/rtx-NYQ:RTX/news/us-approves-billions-in-arms-sales-to-middle-east-countries-updated-019e6a16-0cfb-71f9-a898-5875d8c2e712/); [Defense News 2016](https://defensenews.com/pentagon/2016/12/08/uae-apaches-saudi-chinooks-top-massive-7-9-billion-weapon-sales-plan); [Defense News 2017](https://www.defensenews.com/pentagon/2017/09/13/us-clears-record-total-for-arms-sales-in-fy17/)
- Market reaction to FMS headlines is muted/mixed: after a Saudi arms deal Barron's reported LMT -1.5%, NOC -0.6%, GD -0.2%, LHX flat, RTX +0.5%, partly attributing weakness to trade concerns. — [Barron's via iTiger](https://www-web.itiger.com/news/2535704030)
- A niche Beehiiv newsletter ("Defense News Guru") tracks DSCA announcements by contractor (e.g., "$11 billion RTX contracts"). — [Defense News Guru](https://defense-news-guru.beehiiv.com/p/dsca-announces-11-billion-rtx-contracts)
- **GAO bid protests:** GAO docket pages are public and show outcome/date, e.g., Palantir USG protest B-423684.3 posted Jul 30, 2025, outcome "Dismissed." GAO closed 1,737 cases in FY2025, 359 involving task/delivery orders. — [GAO docket B-423684.3](https://www.gao.gov/docket/b-423684.3); [GAO docket B-423684.1](https://www.gao.gov/docket/b-423684.1); [Watt Tieder FY25 report takeaways](https://watttieder.com/?p=9921)
- Protest-news speed example: Carahsoft filed and withdrew a GAO protest of Palantir's $385M VA win within ~24 hours (Nov 2025), reported by Washington Technology (GovExec). — [Washington Technology](https://www.washingtontechnology.com/companies/2025/11/carahsoft-files-and-withdraws-protest-palantirs-va-win-24-hours/409661)
- Low-quality crypto/aggregator sites also report PLTR protest headlines with stock-move claims (e.g., "+1% pre-market July 24, 2026" on a DIA solicitation withdrawal) — unconfirmed by primary sources; treat as noise. — [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/palantir-forces-dia-withdraw-military-bid/)

### Inferences
- Because the DoD list is posted after the close, the "edge" is in parsing it before the next open (free: war.gov page + RSS/Visualping; paid: HigherGov/Govly/GovTribe-style alerts). Large IDIQ ceilings (e.g., SHIELD's 1,014 + 1,086 awards) are often misread as revenue; Defense Daily/GovConWire context matters.
- DSCA notices are a weak single-event signal for mega-caps (LMT/RTX), per Barron's example, but matter more for mid-caps named as principal contractor (e.g., Stinger -> RTX, PATRIOT -> LMT, THAAD -> LMT).
- GAO dockets are the only place to see protest filing/outcome dates directly; outlets like Inside Defense and Washington Technology surface them within hours.
