## Key Question 2 — Primary data feeds that beat the press (BLS/BEA/Census schedule & APIs, no lockup since 2020; Fed Board calendar; FOMC statement/minutes timing; Treasury refunding; CBO; Federal Register public inspection; H.4.1/H.8; GDPNow, NY Fed Nowcast, Cleveland Fed nowcast; TGA; ICI flows)

### Takeaway
Since June 3, 2020 there has been no media lockup for Labor Department (and Commerce) data — the agency website is the simultaneous first source at 8:30 am ET — so a retail investor hitting the BLS/BEA site or API at release time is on equal footing with wires. The Fed's own calendar gives exact times: FOMC minutes 2:00 pm ET three weeks after the meeting (Nov 18, 2026 for the Oct 27–28 meeting), H.4.1 Thursdays 4:30 pm, H.8 Fridays 4:15 pm; Treasury refunding is Wednesday 8:30 am ET (last: Nov 4, 2026; next: Feb 3, 2027).
