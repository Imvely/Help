## Key Question 1 — Paid insider tech press: The Information, Semafor Tech, Puck, Platformer, Stratechery, Big Technology, Newcomer (price, speed, hit rate)

### Takeaway
The Information is the only outlet in this group with a documented, repeated record of breaking market-moving deal terms (OpenAI/Microsoft) that Reuters and Barron's then relay with "could not independently verify" caveats; it costs roughly $399/yr standard with higher Pro/event tiers. Stratechery, Platformer, Big Technology and Newcomer are primarily analysis/interview products (slow, not scoop-first), while Semafor Tech is a free twice-weekly briefing with occasional scoops.

## Key Question 2 — Semiconductor supply-chain sources (SemiAnalysis, Fabricated Knowledge, Chip Letter, Asianometry, DigiTimes, TrendForce, Counterpoint, IDC/Gartner, Korean & Taiwanese outlets)

### Takeaway
The fastest market-moving semis news now originates in Korean trade press (ETNews, Seoul Economic Daily, THE ELEC/디일렉) and Taiwanese business dailies (Economic Daily News/UDN), is relayed within a day by DigiTimes and Taipei Times, and shows up as MU/NVDA/TSM moves before Bloomberg; TrendForce DRAM/NAND contract-price forecasts are a documented, repeatable MU mover. ## Key Question 3 — AI research/news fast lanes (Import AI, The Batch, Latent Space, AI News/smol.ai, Hacker News, arXiv trackers, lab-insider X accounts) and leak outlets for Apple (Gurman/Power On, MacRumors, 9to5Mac) and Tesla (Electrek, Teslarati)

### Inferences
- AI newsletters do not beat wires on anything tradeable; their value is in interpreting model releases (e.g., GPU demand implications for NVDA/AVGO) within 24–72 hours.
